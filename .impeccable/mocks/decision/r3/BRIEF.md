## Concepts
- BOOKSHELF: links are book spines standing on shelves (the owner's original idea, now in the theme). Spines vary in height/width and cloth colour (theme palette), each with the session number on a small label near the top and the title running vertically; unread = a spot-colour ribbon bookmark hanging from the top of the spine onto a light label or a light tag at its end. A shelf plaque "Sessions 001 – 004". Bookends and empty shelf space waiting for more. The shelf itself drawn in the theme's ornament (Nouveau ironwork brackets / gilt-edged marble shelf / charcoal shelf over the tile floor).
- SALON WALL: links hang as framed miniatures salon-style on the theme's wall, mixed frame sizes and shapes (oval, arched, rectangular) in the theme's frame language, the NEWEST (004) the largest near the centre. Each frame holds a small painted/drawn picture of the link's motif and a small name plate below (number + title + domain). Unread = a small spot-colour ribbon rosette pinned to the frame corner (on a light plate/mat). A few empty picture hooks/nails waiting for future frames.
